SECTION 1. PURPOSE
This policy states the prior-authorization requirements that apply to Drug A when it is requested for Example Condition. It applies to commercial pharmacy benefit plans administered by Example Health Plan.

SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS
Documented diagnosis means a diagnosis recorded in the medical record by the treating prescriber.
Therapy course means a continuous period of treatment with a single agent, measured from the first documented fill.
Inadequate response means a documented failure to reach the treatment target after a completed therapy course.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE CRITERIA
Drug A is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

1. The patient has a documented diagnosis of Example Condition.
2. The patient has completed at least 12 weeks of therapy with Drug A.
3. The patient had an inadequate response to therapy with Drug A after at least 12 weeks.
4. The most recent A1c is below 8.0 percent.
5. Required screening documentation is on file.
6. The duration of the most recent therapy course is documented and is at least 8 weeks.

SECTION 4. EXCLUSIONS
Drug A is not covered when ANY of the following applies:

1. The patient has a documented allergy to Drug A.
2. The request is for an indication that is not listed in Section 1.

SECTION 5. AUTHORIZATION PERIOD
Initial authorization is granted for 12 months. Reauthorization requires documentation that the patient continues to meet the criteria in Section 3.
