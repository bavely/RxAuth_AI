SECTION 3. COVERAGE CRITERIA
Drug C is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

1. The patient has a documented diagnosis of Example Condition B.
2. The patient has completed at least 6 weeks of therapy with Drug B.
3. The prescriber has submitted a written statement explaining why every formulary alternative is expected to be clinically inappropriate for this patient.
4. The most recent LDL cholesterol is below 100.

SECTION 4. EXCLUSIONS
