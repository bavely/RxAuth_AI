SECTION 1. PURPOSE
This policy states the prior-authorization requirements that apply to adalimumab (Humira) when it is requested for Example Inflammatory Condition.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE CRITERIA
Adalimumab is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

1. The patient has a documented diagnosis of Example Inflammatory Condition.
2. The patient has completed at least 12 weeks of therapy with methotrexate.
3. The patient had an inadequate response to therapy with methotrexate after at least 12 weeks.
4. The most recent CRP is above 5.0.
5. Required screening documentation is on file.

SECTION 5. AUTHORIZATION PERIOD
